# Improvements

Ideas to revisit after launch. Not part of the first build.

## Trial abuse

**Context:** Evergrow offers a 1-month free trial with no card required. Without a card on file, nothing stops someone signing up again with a new email each month.

**For now:** don't worry about it. Treat it as a launch-and-watch item.

**If it becomes a problem:**
- Limit to one trial per email address.
- Later, limit to one trial per payment method or device.
- Watch for patterns, such as many new accounts from the same device or network.
- Keep the cost of a trial low (the AI help limit per book already caps spend).

**Related:** when a trial ends, the account goes read-only and progress is kept, so a returning parent can subscribe and continue where they stopped.
